# M6c study: nuclear quantum effects in gas-phase water with MACE-OFF23

Experiment record for MILESTONE_6 "M6c: study" (WORKFLOW "Experiments"). The plan is written and
approved before any production run; the log is appended as work proceeds; the writeup comes last
and its claims cite log entries. Only merged engine code is used (`main` at 9c6096b or later
through a normal milestone); nothing under `crates/` changes. Scripts and configs:
`studies/m6-water-mace/`; run outputs in `studies/m6-water-mace/out/` (git-ignored; trajectories
and MACE-OFF23 exports are never committed). Tables and figures are regenerated by script into
`studies/m6-water-mace/results/` (small JSON, CSV and PNG files, committed).

Scope chosen by the owner (2026-11-02): the briefed study (1), a harmonic cross-check from each
model's own frequencies (2), isotopes (3), and a reduced throughput survey (6). Model-size
sensitivity is left out; the literature appears as a short cited comparison in the writeup only.

Plan approved by the owner (2026-11-02) with: a 48 h wall budget for all production runs, the
machine held free of other heavy work; the P = 32 runs may use CUDA float64; the study directory
`studies/m6-water-mace/` with `out/` ignored.

## Plan

### Questions

1. **Quantum vs classical, MACE-OFF23 vs GFN2-xTB.** How large are the nuclear quantum effects on
   the energy and geometry of one water molecule at 300 K with MACE-OFF23 small, how does the PIMD
   result converge in P, and how do the quantum shifts compare with GFN2-xTB's?
2. **Harmonic cross-check.** Does the PIMD energy at each P follow the exact P-bead result for a
   harmonic molecule with the model's own normal-mode frequencies, and how large is the remaining
   (anharmonic) difference at converged P?
3. **Isotopes.** How do the quantum shifts change for D₂O and HDO (masses only, same model)? In
   HDO, are ⟨r_OH⟩ and ⟨r_OD⟩ resolved?
4. **Throughput.** Time per MD step of the full engine with the in-process MACE backend, for water
   and ethanol at P = 1, 8, 16, 32, on CPU (1 and 4 libtorch threads) and CUDA, float64 and float32;
   and the GFN2-xTB socket path for comparison (water).

### Hypotheses (written before running)

- Energies are compared as **E − V_min** (each model's own minimum energy), because MACE-OFF23 and
  GFN2-xTB have unrelated energy zeros.
- **Q1, energy.** The quantum−classical difference in ⟨E_vir⟩ − V_min is about the harmonic
  zero-point energy minus the classical vibrational energy, ≈ 0.018 Eh (ZPE ≈ 0.021 Eh for
  frequencies near 1 600, 3 700 and 3 800 cm⁻¹, minus 3 k_BT ≈ 0.0029 Eh). It is not converged at
  P = 8 and is within a few per cent of its limit at P = 32.
- **Q1, geometry.** Quantum ⟨r_OH⟩ exceeds classical by 0.01–0.03 bohr for both models; ⟨θ_HOH⟩
  moves by less than 1°. MACE-OFF23 (trained on ωB97M-D3(BJ)) gives a shorter ⟨r_OH⟩ than
  GFN2-xTB (M5: 1.8492 bohr = 0.9786 Å at P = 16), closer to r_e ≈ 0.958 Å plus the quantum
  expansion.
- **Q2.** At every P the PIMD ⟨E_vir⟩ − V_min agrees with the harmonic prediction to within a few
  per cent of the quantum−classical difference, and at converged P it lies below it (anharmonicity
  lowers the zero-point energy of the stretches). The geometry shifts have no harmonic counterpart
  (a harmonic mode does not move its mean), so they are entirely anharmonic.
- **Q3.** The quantum−classical energy difference shrinks roughly as m^(−1/2) of the light atom:
  D₂O/H₂O ≈ 0.7 (harmonic estimate with frequencies near 1 200, 2 750, 2 900 cm⁻¹ for D₂O). In HDO, ⟨r_OH⟩ > ⟨r_OD⟩ by more than 5σ.
- **Q4.** The spike timings hold for the engine (MD step ≈ evaluation time plus a small overhead).
  For water, CUDA pays off only at P ≥ 16 in float64; for ethanol at P ≥ 8.

### Protocol

Common: gas phase, 300 K, dt = 0.5 fs, BAOAB + PILE-L with τ_0 = 20 fs, γ_k = 2ω_k (the M5
protocol, so the xTB numbers are comparable); MACE runs on CPU in float64 with 1 libtorch thread
(energy observables from float64, decision 7), unless a run is moved to CUDA float64 for time
(below). Each run: 20 000 equilibration steps (10 ps), then production; observables every step;
`trajectory.xyz` every 10 steps for the geometry. One seed per run (seed = 1000 + P for H₂O, +100
for D₂O, +200 for HDO, +300 for xTB), recorded in the log.

0. **Pilot (logged).** 2 000 steps at each P of the H₂O series, to measure the time per step on
   this machine with the merged engine. Production lengths are then fixed to fit the budget below
   and written into the log before the production runs start. Default if the budget allows: 200 000
   production steps (100 ps) per run.
1. **H₂O, MACE-OFF23**: P = 1, 4, 8, 16, 32. A second seed at P = 32 to check the error bars.
2. **H₂O, GFN2-xTB**: P = 1 and P = 32 (the M5 run gives P = 16). Same protocol.
3. **Minima and harmonic analysis** (`harmonic.py`, Python, `ml` env for MACE through
   `MACECalculator` on the same model file, `ref` env for tblite): optimise the geometry (forces
   < 1e-6 Eh/bohr), finite-difference Hessian (central differences, step 1e-3 bohr, checked against
   5e-4), mass-weighted normal modes for H₂O, D₂O and HDO; record V_min, the three frequencies, and
   the six near-zero external modes. Then the exact P-bead harmonic energy at 300 K for each P:
   E_P = −∂ ln Z_P / ∂β for each mode, Z_P from the ring-polymer normal-mode eigenvalues
   (ω_k² + ω² with ω_k = 2(P/βħ) sin(πk/P)), plus the external degrees of freedom treated
   classically (translation and rotation: kinetic only, 6 × k_BT/2). The script checks itself: E_1 = k_BT per mode, and E_P → (ħω/2)
   coth(βħω/2) as P → ∞.
4. **D₂O and HDO, MACE-OFF23**: P = 1 and P = 32.
5. **Throughput** (`bench.sh`): fixed 1 000-step runs after a 100-step warm-up, best of 3; water
   and ethanol; P = 1, 8, 16, 32; CPU with 1 and 4 libtorch threads, CUDA; float64 and float32
   (CUDA with `deterministic = true`, the default, and once with `false` at P = 32); xTB socket
   path for water at P = 1, 16, 32. Machine load recorded at start and end. Not run while other
   runs of this study are running.

Analysis (`analyse.py`, regenerates every table and figure): per run ⟨E_vir⟩, ⟨E_prim⟩, ⟨V̄⟩ per
molecule minus V_min; bead-averaged ⟨r_OH⟩ (⟨r_OH⟩ and ⟨r_OD⟩ separately for HDO) and ⟨θ_HOH⟩; r_OH
histogram (25 bins, 1.4–2.4 bohr as M5). Errors by block averaging with the WORKFLOW policy
(2-ps blocks checked against 8-ps blocks, σ the larger). Figures: E_vir − V_min vs 1/P² with the
harmonic curve; r_OH distributions, quantum vs classical, both models; isotope comparison;
throughput vs P.

### Budget and resources

Owner's machine (12 logical CPUs, RTX 4060). Estimated from the spike timings (water, f64, one
thread: about 10, 50, 105, 224 ms per evaluation at P = 1, 8, 16, 32): 200 000 steps at P = 32 on
one CPU thread is ≈ 12 h; the full H₂O series run in parallel (one process per P) ≈ 12–14 h wall.
CUDA float64 at P = 32 (≈ 47 ms per evaluation in the spike) would take ≈ 3 h, so the P = 32 runs
may go to CUDA float64 if the pilot confirms it; results on CUDA float64 agree with CPU float64
to ≤ 1e-14 relative per evaluation (RESULTS.md M6b), far below the statistical errors. Upper limit
for all production runs together: about 48 h wall. If the pilot shows that the default lengths do
not fit, the production steps are shortened (and the larger error bars stated), not the set of P.
At most 6 runs at once (one per physical core), and nothing else heavy on the machine.

### What counts as agreement

- **Engine consistency** (must hold, else stop and investigate): ⟨E_vir⟩ and ⟨E_prim⟩ agree
  within 5σ_c at every P; the P = 1 run gives ⟨E⟩ − V_min close to the classical harmonic value
  6 k_BT (9 k_BT/2 kinetic, 3 k_BT/2 from the three vibrations; the anharmonic correction is
  reported); the second P = 32 seed agrees within 5σ_c.
- **Harmonic cross-check** (Q2): the difference PIMD − harmonic is reported with its σ at each P;
  "agrees with the hypothesis" if |difference| ≤ 5 % of the quantum−classical difference at every
  P. A larger difference is a result, not a failure, if it is constant in P (anharmonicity) rather
  than growing with P (which would point to a problem).
- **MACE vs xTB, isotopes, literature**: reported as numbers with σ; a hypothesis "holds" if the
  sign and size are as stated beyond 5σ. Literature values (equilibrium and vibrationally averaged
  water geometry, anharmonic zero-point energy) are cited with their source in the writeup only.
- **dt bias**: none assumed beyond M5's measurement (no resolved bias at 0.5 fs vs 0.25 fs for
  xTB at P = 16). If a MACE energy difference of interest is below 3 × 1.6e-4 Eh (M5's 5σ_c), one
  dt/2 run at P = 32 is added.

### What would change the plan

A run failing or drifting (energy conservation is not checked by a thermostatted run; use the
E_prim/E_vir consistency and the T_est column) stops the series; the failure is logged. A defect in
the engine goes to QUESTIONS.md and a normal milestone, not into this study. If CUDA float64
results differ from CPU float64 beyond the stated per-evaluation tolerance, the P = 32 runs stay
on CPU.

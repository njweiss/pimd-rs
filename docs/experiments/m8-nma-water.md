# M8b study: nuclear quantum effects on N-methylacetamide in water with MACE-OFF23

Experiment record for MILESTONE_8 "M8b study" (WORKFLOW "Experiments"). The plan is written and
approved before any production run; the log is appended as work proceeds; the writeup comes last
and its claims cite log entries. Only merged engine code is used (`main` at f313da9 or later
through a normal milestone); nothing under `crates/` changes. Scripts and configs:
`studies/m8-nma-water/`; run outputs in `studies/m8-nma-water/out/` (git-ignored by
`/studies/*/out/`; trajectories and MACE-OFF23 exports are never committed). Tables and figures
are regenerated by script into `studies/m8-nma-water/results/` (small JSON, CSV and PNG files,
committed).

Plan approved by the owner (2026-11-07) with: equilibration option (b) (P > 1 runs start from
the P = 1 run's equilibrated configuration, 5 ps of equilibration); a cap of 100 h of GPU wall
time for production, not counting downtime between an interruption and its restart from a
checkpoint; the optional MACE-OFF23 medium run on hold.

## Plan

### Questions

1. **Solute–solvent hydrogen bonds.** How do nuclear quantum effects change the hydration of the
   NMA amide group at 300 K with MACE-OFF23 small: the carbonyl O to water H and the amide H to
   water O site RDFs, hydrogen-bond distances and counts?
2. **Solute geometry.** How do they change the NMA bond lengths (C=O, C–N, N–H) and the amide
   torsion distribution (planarity of the peptide bond)?
3. **Convergence in P.** Which observables are converged at P = 8, 16 and 32, and how does ⟨E_vir⟩
   approach its limit?
4. **float32 energies (decision 6).** Do the float32 energies used in the run bias ⟨V̄⟩ or ⟨E_vir⟩
   compared with a float64 re-evaluation of the same frames?
5. **Throughput.** Time per step of the production runs, against the spike's projection (R6).

### Hypotheses (written before running)

- **Q1, H-bonds.** Quantum delocalisation broadens and lowers the first peak of both site RDFs
  (O_NMA–H_w and H_N–O_w) beyond 5σ, while the peak positions move by less than 0.05 Å and the
  mean H-bond counts per frame change by less than 10 % (competing quantum effects: a longer,
  more polar N–H and C=O against a more delocalised hydrogen). The water–water g_OO first peak is
  lower at P = 32 than at P = 1 (test 8 at 64 waters: i-PI first peak 2.63 at P = 1, 2.50 at
  P = 8).
- **Q2, geometry.** Quantum ⟨r_NH⟩ exceeds classical by 0.01–0.03 Å (M6c water: ⟨r_OH⟩ +0.027
  bohr = 0.014 Å for MACE in gas phase; an H-bonded N–H softens, so a larger shift is plausible),
  and the N–H distribution is about twice as wide. C=O and C–N shift by less than 0.01 Å. The
  amide torsion stays trans (|ω| > 150° in more than 99 % of samples at every P), with a wider
  distribution at P = 32 than at P = 1 (the N–H wag and the torsion couple).
- **Q3.** The structural observables (site RDFs, bond lengths) agree between P = 16 and P = 32
  within 5σ_c; ⟨E_vir⟩ is not converged at P = 8 and changes by a few per cent of the
  quantum−classical difference between P = 16 and P = 32. The quantum−classical ⟨E_vir⟩
  difference is about the harmonic zero-point minus classical vibrational energy, dominated by
  the waters: test 8 measured 0.0140 Eh per molecule between P = 8 and P = 1 at 64 waters.
- **Q4.** No float32 bias: the float32 − float64 difference in ⟨V̄⟩ and ⟨E_vir⟩ per molecule is
  smaller than one fifth of the statistical σ of the same run (a float32 bead energy carries
  ≈ 1e-3 Eh of rounding at |E| ≈ 1.7e4 Eh, test 7: 0.106 eV = 3.9e-3 Eh worst, random in sign).
- **Q5.** The spike's projection holds within ±30 %: 76, 487, ≈ 975 and 1 967 ms per step at
  P = 1, 8, 16, 32 (chunks of 8).

### Model and system

- **MACE-OFF23 small** (`ff_models/mace_off23/MACE-OFF23_small.model`, sha256 165cce4c…),
  exported to float64 with `crates/pimd-torch/scripts/export_mace.py` into `ff_models/exports/`

  `deterministic = true`.
- **NMA + 216 waters**: the box `nma_w216` of `crates/pimd-cli/tests/data/m8_boxes.json` (660
  atoms, cubic L = 18.760 Å, symbols and masses from the JSON; NMA is atoms 0–11, order C H H H C O
  N H C H H H). The box was equilibrated by OpenMM with amber14/TIP3P and rigid waters (50 ps,
  `gen_m8_boxes.py`), so the first MACE equilibration also relaxes the water geometry.
  `make_configs.py` writes it as `out/start/nma_w216.xyz` and the configs read it through
  `system.positions_xyz`; masses go inline as `system.masses_amu`.

### Protocol

Common: NVT, 300 K, dt = 0.5 fs, BAOAB + PILE-L with τ_0 = 100 fs and γ_k = 2ω_k (as the spike
and test 8); float32 CUDA, `beads_per_graph` set in every config (decision 7): c = 1 at P = 1,
c = 8 at P = 8, 16, 32 (≈ 200 k edges per graph, the spike's safe size);
`PYTORCH_CUDA_ALLOC_CONF=expandable_segments:True` in the environment of every run.
Observables every step (`stride = 1`); `trajectory.xyz` (all beads) every 40 steps (20 fs; 1 000
production frames, about 1.4 MB per frame at P = 32, ≈ 4 GB for the whole study); checkpoint
every 1 000 steps (an interruption loses at most ≈ 35 min at P = 32; restarts are bitwise, D-3).
Runs one after another, never concurrently (Phase A: concurrent CUDA contexts lowered total
throughput), with the GPU otherwise idle. Seeds 8801, 8808, 8816, 8832 and 8932 (second P = 32),
recorded in the log.

0. **Pilot (logged).** 200 steps at P = 1, 8, 16, 32 from the box: ms per step and peak GPU
   memory (`nvidia-smi`) against the spike's numbers. A time more than 1.5× the spike's at any P
   means a spill into shared memory: that P is re-piloted with c = 4 and the faster c is used.
   Production lengths below are confirmed or shortened in the log before production starts.
1. **P = 1** (classical nuclei): 20 000 equilibration steps (10 ps) from the box, then 40 000
   production steps (20 ps).
2. **P = 8, 16, 32 and the second P = 32 seed.** Option (b) (owner, 2026-11-07); (a) not used:
   - (a) as the brief's outline: each run starts from the box with 10 ps of equilibration, then
     20 ps of production (91 h in total, spike R6);
   - (b) **chosen**: each starts from an equilibrated MACE configuration taken from the
     P = 1 run, with 5 ps (10 000 steps) of equilibration, then 20 ps of production. P = 8, 16 and
     the first P = 32 start from the P = 1 frame at the start of production; the second P = 32
     starts from the P = 1 frame at the end of production (20 ps later, so the two seeds also
     differ in their starting structure). All beads start at the frame's positions; the ring
     polymer's internal modes thermalise within a few τ_0 under PILE-L, and the 10 ps of P = 1
     equilibration has already removed the TIP3P structure. Saves ≈ 15 h (76 h in total). The
     first-half/second-half check below tests whether 5 ps was enough.
3. **float64 re-evaluation** (`reeval_f64.py`, pixi `ml` env, mace's ASE `MACECalculator` in
   float64 on CUDA, periodic, bead by bead, as `gen_mace_off23_nma.py`): 50 frames of each run,
   evenly spaced over production (400 fs apart). For each frame, the bead-averaged float64 V̄ and
   the float64 E_vir (E-3 with float64 forces) are compared with the engine's `observables.csv`
   row of the same step. Run after all production runs (≈ 4 450 bead evaluations over the five
   runs, about 1–1.5 h on the GPU).
4. **Optional, on hold (owner, 2026-11-07): model check.** One classical P = 1 run with MACE-OFF23 medium (float32
   CUDA, c = 1; spike: ≈ 5 h per 60 000 steps), same protocol as run 1, comparing the site RDFs
   and NMA geometry with small. Revisited after the main results.

Analysis (`analyse.py`, regenerates every table and figure from `out/`; atoms identified from the
symbols and the first frame's bonds):
- **Energies**: ⟨E_vir⟩, ⟨E_prim⟩, ⟨V̄⟩ per molecule (217 molecules), the estimated temperature.
- **Site RDFs**, bead-averaged (computed per bead with the minimum image C-2, then averaged over
  beads, as test 8's g_OO), bins of 0.05 Å on [0, 9) Å (L/2 = 9.38 Å): O_NMA–H_w, O_NMA–O_w,
  H_N–O_w, N–O_w, and the water–water g_OO and g_OH; first-peak position (parabola through the
  highest bin and its neighbours), height, first minimum and coordination number.
- **Hydrogen bonds**, per bead per frame, geometric criteria: C=O···H–O_w with r(O_NMA···O_w)
  < 3.5 Å and ∠(O_w–H_w, O_w···O_NMA) < 30°; N–H···O_w with r(N···O_w) < 3.5 Å and
  ∠(N–H, N···O_w) < 30°. Mean count per frame and mean H···acceptor distance; the counts'
  sensitivity to the cutoffs (3.3 and 3.7 Å, 20° and 40°) reported.
- **NMA geometry**, per bead: r(C=O), r(C–N), r(N–H) means and histograms; the amide torsion
  ω = C_methyl–C–N–C_methyl and O=C–N–H histograms.
- **Errors**: block averaging with the WORKFLOW policy (σ = the largest of 1, 2 and 4 ps blocks;
  structural observables blocked per frame). Production halves (0–10 ps, 10–20 ps) compared for
  every reported observable (equilibration check).
- **Throughput**: ms per step from each run's wall time.
- Figures: site RDFs at P = 1 and P = 32; r(N–H) and ω distributions vs P; ⟨E_vir⟩ vs 1/P².

### What counts as agreement

- **Run health** (must hold, else stop and investigate): no NMA bond longer than 1.3× its
  starting length and no proton transfer (every H within 1.3 Å of its own heavy atom) in any bead
  of any frame; estimated temperature within 5σ of 300 K; ⟨E_vir⟩ and ⟨E_prim⟩ agree within 5σ_c
  at every P.
- **Reproducibility**: the two P = 32 runs agree within 5σ_c on every reported observable; the two
  production halves of every run agree within 5σ_c (otherwise the equilibration was too short:
  the first 5 ps of production is discarded and the shortening stated).
- **Hypotheses**: a hypothesis "holds" if the sign and size are as stated beyond 5σ (or, for a
  "less than" statement, the measured value plus 2σ is below the bound).
- **float32 bias (Q4)**: no bias if |mean float32 − float64 difference| < σ(⟨·⟩)/5 for ⟨V̄⟩ and
  ⟨E_vir⟩ at P = 1 and P = 32; otherwise the export follow-up of decision 6 is proposed as a
  normal milestone, and the energies of this study are reported with the measured correction.
- **dt bias**: none assumed (spike R5: no resolved energy drift at 0.5 or 1.0 fs in NVE). If a
  quantum−classical difference of interest is below 3σ_c, the writeup says it is not resolved; no
  dt/2 run is planned.

### What would change the plan

A failed health check stops the series; the failure is logged. A spill or a time per step more
than 1.5× the pilot's during production pauses the run (restart from its checkpoint after the
GPU is free). An engine defect goes to QUESTIONS.md and a normal milestone, not into this study.
If the pilot shows the budget does not fit the cap, production is shortened (and the larger
error bars stated) before the set of P is cut; the second P = 32 seed is the first to go.
